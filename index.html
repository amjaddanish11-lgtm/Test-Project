FAQ

Questions business owners ask us.

Can you make my business logo in neon?

Yes — that's our specialty. We print your full-colour logo on a 3mm acrylic base cut to shape, then trace it with an LED neon outline. Logos, mascots, characters and lettering all work.

How much will my sign cost?

Starting prices are AED 550 (30cm), AED 950 (60cm) and AED 1150 (100cm). The final price depends on your logo's complexity, colours, acrylic shape and installation. Send your logo on WhatsApp and you'll get an exact quote with a mockup.

Can you make Arabic and English designs?

Yes. We produce Arabic, English and mixed designs — including Arabic calligraphy combined with logo artwork, like the character pieces in our gallery.

I only have an idea, no logo file. Can you help?

Absolutely. Describe your idea on WhatsApp and we'll design the artwork and send you a mockup before anything goes to production.

What file should I send?

Whatever you have — PNG, JPG, PDF, AI or even a photo of a sketch. High-resolution files give the cleanest print, but we can work from almost anything.

How long does production take?

Timelines depend on size and complexity. We confirm production and delivery dates together with your mockup and quote on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver and install?

Yes — delivery and professional installation can be arranged across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the wider UAE.

Is it for indoor or outdoor use?

Most brand pieces are designed for interiors, where they look their best. Tell us where you plan to mount yours and we'll recommend the right setup.
