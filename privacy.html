Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

High Quality Door Handles is the sales page of an independent seller of door handles in Toronto. For any privacy question, contact amjaddan66@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send an order enquiry, we receive the name, email address, optional phone number, city or postal code, requested items and message you provide. Please do not send payment-card details or other sensitive information through the form.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, quote shipping and complete your order. We do not sell your personal information. Order information is kept only as needed to handle the enquiry or order and meet any applicable record-keeping requirements.

Form processing and delivery

The order form sends your enquiry through FormSubmit to our email inbox. FormSubmit and the email provider process the information to deliver and store the message under their own privacy practices. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy. You can email us directly instead of using the form. If shipping is arranged, delivery information needed to send the order may be shared with the carrier.

Cookies and tracking

This static page does not include advertising pixels or analytics scripts and does not set cookies itself. The external form service may use its own security checks when you submit an enquiry.

Questions or requests

To ask about your information, request a correction or request deletion, email amjaddan66@gmail.com. We will respond and help where possible, subject to any information we must retain for order records or legal requirements.
